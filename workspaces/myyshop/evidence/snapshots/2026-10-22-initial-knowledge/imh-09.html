To make it easier, here are our favorite TikTok tools for finding influencers.

Advanced TikTok Influencer Filtering

Modash lets marketers filter the entire TikTok creator universe using audience and creator-level criteria like age, gender, location, engagement rate, growth trends, and keyword signals.

Using lookalike search, Modash identifies TikTok creators whose audiences resemble your top-performing partners. This helps marketers rapidly scale what already works by finding hundreds of similar creators.

MyyShop’s livestream support helps brands connect those pieces instead of treating LIVE as a separate experiment.

For products that need explanation, demonstration, or urgency-driven selling, this is one of MyyShop’s stronger TikTok advantages.

MyyShop’s content support focuses on TikTok-native short-form strategy, UGC-style videos, creator-style content, product storytelling, hooks, and A/B testing. This makes MyyShop a stronger fit for brands that want TikTok content to become a performance asset, not just a branding deliverable.

That is the right direction for TikTok Shop, where smaller creators can outperform larger ones when their audience trust, content format, and product fit are stronger.
