Advertising Disclosure

While it is possible to participate in influencer marketing on the cheap, building relationships with influencers gradually and organically, it’s challenging work. If you factor in the time it takes for you (or an employee) to create the necessary relationships, it can also be relatively expensive.

Many influencer marketing software tools make this process far more straightforward and are likely to save you money.

Although these software tools (also known as software platforms) initially just focused on influencer search and discovery, they have widened the services they now offer. Indeed, you can run virtually an entire influencer campaign using one or a combination of these apps or other types of software.

We've outlined the use of third-party solutions in our 2026 Influencer Marketing Benchmark data.

According to our data, 19% of brands outsource creator discovery and vetting, 12.5% outsource talent management/long-term creator partnerships, while a smaller percentage, 9.7%, outsource affiliate or influencer commerce management.

As you can see, software runs the influencer marketing sphere. This guide looks at what the best solutions have to offer.

1. Upfluence

Upfluence analyzes nearly 3 million influencers with a collective reach of 82 billion followers. The software indexes and updates all their profiles in real-time, with every piece of content analyzed for reach and engagement. 

A new tool, Live Capture, attempts to find the best influencers for your brand. It assumes that your customers are the best advocates for your brand and can become ambassadors to promote your products.

eCommerce businesses can connect their online store to Upfluence’s software to discover influencers who visit their website and activate them through dedicated campaigns

Read More

Brands and agencies can search through Upfluence to find influencers using as many keywords as necessary. You can place a relative weight on each keyword. Upfluence covers all major social media platforms: Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, Twitter, Pinterest, Twitch, and TikTok.

Key Features: Influencer Search & Discovery, Relationship Management, Campaign Management, Third Party Analytics, Automated Recruiting, Influencer Lifecycle Management, Team Collaboration Tools, Content Review, Campaign Reporting, Audience Analysis, E-commerce Tools, Product/Gifting Tools, Payment Processing, Social Listening, Affiliate Management, Affiliate Campaigns,

Channels: Instagram, Youtube, Facebook, Twitch, Tiktok, Twitter, Pinterest, Blogs

2. Modash

If you’re searching for robust influencer marketing software, Modash might be your perfect match. Modash excels in three core areas: discovery, insights, and monitoring, covering Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok. It’s designed to streamline the process of finding and working with influencers, ensuring your campaigns hit the mark.

The first feature, discovery, allows you to search for influencers based on specific criteria, such as location, follower count, engagement rate, and interests. Modash further aids your efforts by providing filters to search for influencers by gender, language, and even specific keywords in their bio or content, making it incredibly versatile and precise.

Read More

Understanding the performance and authenticity of influencers is crucial. Modash offers detailed performance analytics, including engagement rates and audience demographics. You can also detect fake followers, ensuring that the influencers you choose have genuine and engaged audiences. 

This tool analyzes publicly available information, employing image recognition and text analysis to build comprehensive profiles and analytics. Whether you’re examining an influencer’s growth trends or their engagement metrics, Modash equips you with the insights needed to make informed decisions.

Navigating through the Modash influencer marketing software is surprisingly straightforward. The software allows you to create lists of potential influencers, export their contact details, and seamlessly integrate with email automation tools like Mailchimp or CRMs like HubSpot. This feature simplifies outreach and helps you manage your influencer relationships efficiently.

3. Creator.co Review

Creator.co is an AI-powered influencer marketing platform built for brands and agencies that want to find, recruit, manage, and measure creators at scale. Teams can search 400M+ global creator profiles using criteria including age, gender, language, interests, location, audience demographics, brand affinity, content style, engagement quality, and performance data. 

London AI reduces the manual work involved in campaign planning and recruitment by developing campaign creative briefs, identifying high-fit creators, and building personalized outreach sequences with automated follow-ups. Once creators are activated, teams can manage campaigns, communications, content and approvals, usage rights, payments, and affiliate programs from one centralized platform.

Creator.co also connects creator activity to measurable business outcomes. Integrations with Instagram, TikTok, TikTok Shop, and YouTube centralize social performance, while Google Analytics, Shopify, and major affiliate network integrations connect creator activity to website traffic, conversions, and sales. Helping teams identify the creators and content driving results and scale what works.

4. Brandwatch Influencer Marketing Software

Brandwatch is a versatile influencer marketing tool that provides a wealth of resources to assist with your influencer marketing strategies. With a large global database of over 30 million creators, Brandwatch allows for a highly targeted approach to finding the right influencers across multiple social media platforms such as YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok.

A key focus of Brandwatch is managing and maintaining influencer relationships. It provides a central hub for storing crucial information such as contracts and payment details, helping to organize and streamline your influencer interactions. This approach contributes to a more efficient and effective relationship management process.

Read More

Another beneficial feature of Brandwatch is its robust campaign success measurement tools. The platform is designed to deliver real-time reports and analytics to help monitor deliverables and assess the return on investment (ROI) of your campaigns. This provides valuable insights into how your influencer marketing activities are performing.

Brandwatch also offers a comprehensive influencer discovery tool, which assists in identifying the most relevant influencers for your specific needs. By tracking and analyzing data from millions of global creators, it can simplify the task of finding the perfect influencer for your brand campaign or agency.

Additionally, the platform excels at managing influencer directories. It facilitates secure recording of influencer contact information, storage of contracts, and collection of additional first-party data. An automated email tracking feature is also available to help keep track of all correspondence with influencers.

5. SARAL

SARAL approaches influencer marketing from an operational angle that many platforms overlook, focusing less on campaign management and more on building a repeatable system for acquiring customers through creators.

It is particularly relevant for eCommerce and DTC brands that rely on a steady flow of new customers and content, where influencer marketing needs to tie directly into sales, not just visibility.

A key strength of the platform is its handling of the transition from discovery to activation. Instead of stopping at influencer lists, SARAL moves creators into structured outreach sequences, tracks conversations, and converts them into affiliates with trackable revenue.

Read More

This creates a clearer path from “found a creator” to “generated a sale,” which is where many influencer tools fall short for commerce-focused teams.

SARAL also leans heavily into efficiency. The platform is built to remove repetitive tasks such as follow-ups, shipping coordination, and code generation, allowing smaller teams to run programs that would typically require significantly more resources. For brands managing product seeding at scale, this becomes especially important, as logistics and communication can quickly become bottlenecks.

Another notable aspect is how SARAL treats influencer programs as a content engine. Instead of relying on branded shoots, brands can continuously source creator-generated content and feed it into paid channels. This creates a loop where influencer activity supports both organic reach and paid performance, helping brands combat creative fatigue in ad platforms.

For brands that view influencer marketing as a core part of their acquisition strategy rather than a supporting channel, SARAL offers a more structured and commerce-aligned approach.

6. MyyShop

If you’re searching for influencer marketing software built around creator commerce, MyyShop is a strong fit for brands that want creator content to connect directly to product discovery, TikTok Shop growth, affiliate performance, and sales-oriented campaign execution. It is less of a classic influencer discovery database and more of a creator activation platform, designed to help brands match with creators, manage campaign participation, support product promotion, and turn creator activity into trackable commercial outcomes.

Read More

The first strength is its TikTok Shop and social commerce focus. MyyShop supports creator-driven growth, livestream commerce, performance-based campaigns, and TikTok Shop operations, giving brands a more commerce-ready structure than a simple influencer outreach tool. Its brand-side workflow covers store setup, compliance, product listings, pricing strategy, inventory and logistics alignment, and performance tracking, which makes the software especially relevant for brands that need influencer marketing to support actual selling infrastructure, not just awareness.

Creator matching is another major part of the platform. MyyShop uses AI trained on large-scale product and creator data to connect creators with relevant brand opportunities. This matters because creator fit in social commerce is rarely just about audience size. Product category, content format, audience behavior, platform strength, and commercial potential all influence whether a creator can move products. MyyShop’s structure supports that logic by combining AI-powered matching with creator qualification, account evaluation, and opportunity recommendations based on social account data, content type, follower count, and view performance.

The creator-side workflow is built for scale. Creators can join even without an existing follower base, which gives brands access to long-tail creators, UGC creators, and early-stage talent that may be useful for product seeding, testing, and content volume. Campaign opportunities are organized around content creation, product promotion, and affiliate promotion. Creators can apply to multiple campaigns, submit content according to advertiser guidelines, receive revision requests, and move toward payout once the work is approved. For brands, that creates a structured campaign pipeline instead of relying on manual DMs, scattered creator emails, or one-off influencer negotiations.

MyyShop also gives influencer campaigns a clearer monetization layer. Creators can earn through content creation fees, promotion fees, brand partnership shares, affiliate commissions, and exposure-based rewards through MyyFinds. That last piece is especially interesting because it allows creators to earn from product views, not only clicks or sales. For marketers, this creates a more flexible way to evaluate creator contribution across the full commerce funnel, from discovery and engagement to conversion.

The platform’s Free Value Report adds another useful layer for creator evaluation. It analyzes niche positioning, audience profile, follower spending power, brand preferences, engagement behavior, commercial persona health, and benchmark gaps against top creators in the same niche. That gives marketers a more practical view of creator potential, especially when campaign success depends on product fit and purchase intent rather than follower count alone.

Navigating MyyShop as influencer marketing software feels most valuable when the goal is creator-powered commerce. It brings together AI matching, TikTok Shop support, content workflows, affiliate promotion, creator payouts, product-view monetization, and campaign performance tracking into one connected system. For brands that want influencer marketing to produce usable content, measurable exposure, and commerce-linked outcomes, MyyShop offers a more operationally grounded approach than platforms built mainly for creator search and outreach.

7. Humanz

Founded only in 2019, Humanz is one of the newer influencer marketing software tools. It’s a powerful tool that relies a lot on artificial intelligence, yet not at the expense of user-friendliness.  

Some of its most noteworthy features include its influencer marketplace and accurate insights. Not only will it help you to connect with the right influencers quickly (you’re also free to work with other influencers and invite them to the Humanz platform), but it also uses proprietary artificial intelligence to analyze creators based on their connection with their true followers. This way, you can predict their expected influence more accurately and avoid fraud. 

Read More

Once you’ve identified the most suitable influencers for your brand, Humanz will also help you to create better briefs. Then, as another safety measure, it offers an easy-to-use feedback and approval system so that you can view the content that influencers plan to share and the context in which it will appear.

8. Stellar

Best for: Brands, agencies, and e-commerce teams using Shopify or WooCommerce that want faster influencer vetting, stronger brand safety, and better campaign intelligence to predict and track ROI and EMV.

Pricing: Starts at €299 per month, with modular plans built so you pay only for the capabilities and scale your team truly needs.

Channels: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Twitch, X (Twitter), Facebook, and Snapchat, with automated content tracking across all relevant content formats.

Stellar is built for teams that want influencer marketing to be more measurable, more credible, and easier to scale. Instead of juggling disconnected tools, manual checks, and spreadsheet-based reporting, brands and agencies can manage creator discovery, profile analysis, campaign execution, forecasting, and reporting in one place.

Read More

The setup adapts to different workflows, team structures, and reporting needs, while a central dashboard gives teams a more holistic view – connecting planning, execution, performance, and learnings in a single system.

On the discovery side, Stellar helps teams find stronger creator fits faster by combining a database of 440 million creators with AI-enhanced social listening and competitor benchmarking.

This makes it easier to surface creators already generating organic traction in a category, identify relevant voices earlier, and build more informed shortlists with less wasted budget. Thanks to its smart search engine, teams can save up to 60% of the time usually spent on creator research.

Just as importantly, discovery in Stellar is built for collaboration. Creator shortlists can be shared directly with internal stakeholders or, in an agency setup, with clients, so they can review, approve, and comment on creators online without needing their own Stellar access.

Profile analysis goes far beyond follower counts. Stellar helps marketing and project teams vet creators more intelligently through audience authenticity checks, audience and location insights, past partnership visibility, and brand safety.

Its AI-powered Brand Safety feature analyzes textual content across every supported social network, in any language, and across years of historical posts. It classifies risk across 11 categories, including threats, violence, self-harm, sexual content, politics, drugs, alcohol, tobacco, war, controversial topics, and coded language such as “algospeak.”

Each flagged item receives a severity score from 0 to 9, helping teams identify potential reputational issues before budget is committed.

Stellar also supports more confident planning. With predictive analytics, marketers can forecast likely campaign performance, estimate ROI and EMV, and make smarter decisions before content goes live.

Once campaigns are running, the platform keeps execution organized through centralized briefs and deliverables, creator coordination, tracking links, promo codes, and shipping workflows. It also preserves campaign knowledge across teams over time, making it easier to learn, optimize, and improve from one activation to the next.

Where Stellar stands out most is measurement and reporting. By combining automated content tracking, attribution, social listening, and sentiment analysis, the platform helps teams see the effects of both paid and unpaid efforts, understand how the audience reacts, and measure the overall discussion about a campaign, including free content and competitor activity.

Reporting is highly customizable, so teams can structure outputs around the metrics, formats, data inputs, and stakeholder views they actually need. For enterprise clients, Stellar can also connect to internal business intelligence environments via API, enabling influencer marketing data to flow into existing dashboards and decision systems.

The result is a clearer, more connected view of ROI, EMV, efficiency, and what truly drove performance.

From Software to Tangible Results

Influencer marketing has evolved into a structured, data-driven channel where execution quality directly impacts ROI. Platforms no longer serve as optional add-ons but as operational infrastructure that enables brands to move faster, make better decisions, and measure outcomes with precision.

From discovery to reporting, the ability to centralize workflows and insights has become a defining factor in campaign success.

The real differentiator, however, lies in how effectively these tools are used. Strong results come from aligning platform capabilities with clear objectives, consistent testing, and disciplined performance tracking.

Brands that treat influencer marketing as a repeatable system rather than a series of one-off campaigns are the ones that scale efficiently, reduce wasted spend, and build long-term competitive advantage.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the benefits of using an influencer marketing software tool?

Most influencer marketing software tools make it easier to build relationships with influencers and it can even save you money. While many of these software solutions at the beginning just concentrated on influencer search and discovery, these tools have now expanded their services. As a matter of fact, you can now practically run an entire influencer campaign with the help of just one of these software solutions. Nowadays, these tools can also help you with relationship management, third-party analytics and influencer content amplification.

What makes Open Influence good?

Open Influence boasts a very powerful image recognition feature. Its AI software uses image recognition to label images with searchable descriptors. This means that you will only see images that you actually want to see. It has indexed more than 20 million recognized keywords for searches based on interests. What is more, it only indexes engaged users and not whole audiences. Though, to unlock this useful feature, it will cost you $500 per month for the Premium plan.

How can you complete an influencer marketing campaign quickly?

An influencer marketplace like Creator.co can really speed up the process of executing campaigns. It uses its own AI Agent, London AI, to go through influencer data from different sources to determine whose audiences are the best fit for campaigns. So, you can save valuable time by not having to go through lists of influencers as it does it for you.

Is NeoReach easy to use?

NeoReach is easy to use thanks to its clean interface. Even with all the information that you get with each profile, it is still very straightforward to work with the search results. Though, it is mostly aimed at big companies with large marketing departments.
