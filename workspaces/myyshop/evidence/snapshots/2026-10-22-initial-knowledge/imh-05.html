The platform now connects AI-powered creator search, outreach, campaign workflows, affiliate links, product gifting, payments, and sales attribution in one workspace.

2. Modash

Best For: Brands of all sizes

Channels: YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Shopify

Pricing: Customized, plans start from $99/month

If you’re looking for a powerful tool to elevate your influencer marketing efforts, Modash is a platform worth considering. With a focus on finding, analyzing, and tracking creators across Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube, Modash offers a robust solution for brands aiming to optimize their influencer marketing campaigns. 

Trusted by over 1,500 marketing teams at globally recognized brands, Modash is designed to help you find the right creators, analyze their performance, and track campaign success—all in one place.

Read More

Modash boasts an impressive database of over 250 million influencer profiles across Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube. Whether you’re targeting niche micro-influencers or large-scale creators, Modash ensures that you’ll never run out of options.

The platform’s advanced filters allow you to narrow down your search by follower count, engagement rate, location, language, and more. If you’ve already worked with successful influencers, you can even use the “Lookalikes” feature to find similar profiles that align with your brand’s goals.

One of the standout features of Modash is its ability to provide detailed insights into an influencer’s audience and performance. You no longer have to rely on surface-level metrics. 

Modash gives you access to data such as fake follower rates, audience demographics (age, gender, location), and the influencer’s content performance. This level of analysis ensures that you make informed decisions when selecting influencers, helping you avoid partnerships that don’t align with your target audience.

Modash streamlines the often tedious process of content collection and campaign tracking. The platform automatically gathers all influencer content, including stories, and tracks their performance without requiring influencer authentication.

You can set specific hashtags, mentions, or keywords, and Modash will collect relevant posts for you. Additionally, Modash offers integration with Shopify, allowing you to manage discount codes and track revenue performance directly from the platform. This makes it easy to assess the ROI of your influencer campaigns in real time.

Managing influencer relationships can be a complex task, especially as your campaigns scale. Modash simplifies this by offering tools to organize and manage your influencer lists. 

You can save shortlisted influencers, unlock contact details, and send emails directly from the platform. Custom tags, notes, and document attachments further enhance your ability to manage each collaboration efficiently.

The platform’s integration with Gmail ensures that your influencer communications remain seamless and centralized.

Tourlane, a luxury travel planning company based in Berlin, partnered with Modash to enhance their influencer marketing efforts. Facing the challenge of selecting the most impactful influencers from a pool of 250-300 prospects each month, Tourlane sought to manage and scale their influencer campaigns more effectively. 

They turned to Modash for its comprehensive data-driven tools to optimize influencer selection and campaign management.

Modash provided Tourlane with a robust platform to vet influencers using both qualitative and quantitative data. Tourlane utilized a flexible scorecard that assessed key metrics like fake follower rate, engagement rate, and audience demographics, ensuring they chose influencers who could deliver high performance. 

This approach allowed Tourlane to maintain a focused campaign with 8-15 high-quality influencers per month, each embarking on curated trips designed to showcase Tourlane’s offerings authentically.

Through this strategic partnership, Tourlane attributed 5-10% of their bookings to influencer marketing, with their best week seeing a 10% contribution. Additionally, their Instagram following grew from 3,500 to 34,000 in just 18 months, significantly boosting both brand awareness and sales. 

As Georgia Humphries, Senior Partnership & Influencer Manager at Tourlane, noted:

Unlike traditional influencer tools that focus on creator search or outreach, Resonate is structured as a full workflow engine that connects strategy, content production, and paid media distribution.

At its core, the platform leverages over 1.5B data points and analyzes more than 25M influencer posts to guide both creator selection and creative direction, reducing reliance on manual vetting and guesswork.

It evaluates influencers based on audience composition, engagement patterns, and content context using image, video, and text recognition. This allows brands to identify creators not just by demographics, but by how their content performs and aligns with campaign objectives.

The platform also incorporates “Creator Intelligence,” a scoring system that ranks influencers based on customizable KPIs such as engagement rate, conversion impact, and content quality, enabling more consistent performance benchmarking across campaigns.

On the execution side, Resonate centralizes campaign workflows, including briefing, creator onboarding, approvals, and asset management, within a single dashboard.

Automated outreach and opt-in flows reduce onboarding time, while collaboration tools allow brands, creators, and internal teams to manage campaigns without relying on external systems. Performance tracking spans both organic and paid media, with real-time reporting on metrics such as impressions, engagement, conversions, and carted value.

A key differentiator is Resonate’s content scaling capabilities. Through tools like Resonate Studio, brands can edit, adapt, and redistribute influencer content across multiple formats, including paid social, CTV, digital out-of-home, and display advertising.

This enables a single creator campaign to function as a multi-channel content engine, with multivariate testing used to identify top-performing assets and scale them accordingly. The result is a closed-loop system that shows what sold, who sold it, and when payouts clear—without juggling half-a-dozen plugins.

Creator & Audience Discovery

Engagement, audience overlap, and product-fit scores sit next to each profile, while AI matching pre-filters for KPIs like conversion likelihood or average order value. Multi-channel support means the same search pulls in Instagram Reels talent, YouTube reviewers, or Reddit niche experts, giving teams a single hunter-dashboard instead of hopping between tools.

Campaign Workflows & Sample Logistics

Once prospects are shortlisted, MyyShop’s seeding module can ship products directly from brand inventory or vetted suppliers. Creators receive track-and-trace sample IDs, upload unboxing content, and auto-attach promotional links generated inside the brief. Fast delivery times (often under a week) keep momentum high and feed the algorithm more data to refine future matches.

Affiliate Links, Livestreams, and TikTok Shop Sync

Every approved post carries performance-based links that reconcile inside TikTok Shop. Brands see real-time click-to-checkout funnels, while creators see dollar equivalents on their dashboards—critical when payments arrive roughly 15 days after a first sale. Livestream scheduling slots neatly into the same calendar, so a brand can pair a top-tier host with long-tail affiliates, maximizing both reach and conversion in one go.

Zero Fees & Faster Payouts for Creators

Unlike marketplaces that skim 10–20 % off the top, MyyShop keeps creator commissions intact. Nano influencers can sign up with zero followers and still earn the full percentage on tracked sales, a policy that has helped 86 % of active users hit growth milestones sooner than on competing networks. Payouts can clear in as little as 15 days, which gives creators faster access to earnings than TikTok’s standard settlement cadence.

AI Analytics & Value Reporting

For brands, the real differentiator is the reporting layer. MyyShop’s dashboards score creative assets against benchmarks, flag high-performing niches, and suggest next-best products for each creator. Teams can export a “Free Value Report” to forecast ROAS before expanding budgets, ensuring media dollars go to SKUs and talent that actually convert.

Enterprise Backing & Scale Potential

Launched by DHgate, MyyShop arrived with a catalog of 2.26 million products and more than 1.66M onboarded influencers, giving it a commerce infrastructure advantage that smaller SaaS competitors, traditional influencer software, and standalone creator databases simply cannot replicate. 

This positions MyyShop less as a point solution and more as a global creator commerce ecosystem that connects brands, products, cross-border logistics, creator activation, and monetization in one operating layer.

On the ecosystem side, that scale gives brands depth across categories like fast-fashion jewelry, lifestyle products, beauty, and niche electronics, while the AI layer helps keep discovery and campaign execution manageable. Instead of asking teams to source products in one place, find creators in another, manage samples separately, and track sales through another tool, MyyShop brings those workflows closer together inside a commerce-first platform.

8. SARAL

Best For:Brands that want to build a predictable, ROI-first influencer program across seeding, affiliates, and partnership ads.

Channels: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube

Pricing: Pricing starts at $3,600 per quarter, with a 20% discount on annual contracts.

SARAL’s strongest operating advantage is connecting creator recruitment to the work that follows a product shipment. For ecommerce teams running continuous seeding, its delivery-triggered communication and affiliate workflows help managers move promising relationships toward published content, repeat collaborations, and measurable sales.

Read More

Discovery indexes public creator profiles across Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube, pulling available public contact information into searchable lists. Keyword and geographic searches support recruitment, while SIA Picks recommends creators resembling existing partners. SIA also drafts personalized outreach using recent posts, bios, and highlights. Sending capacity requires planning: emails are spaced several minutes apart, and the recommended ceiling is 100 emails per inbox daily.

Campaign Mode turns creator lists into separate workflows with customizable stages, dates, budgets, and analytics. Managers can add stages such as Content Approved or Payment Sent, create electronically signed agreements from campaign-specific templates, and review submissions before publication. Requested edits move content into Changes Requested; resubmissions return to Pending Review. This gives the team a practical control point between recruitment and delivery.

The documented Shopify and WooCommerce connection runs through GoAffPro, installed in the store and linked to SARAL with an API access token. That connection supports gifting, discount codes, affiliate links, and sales tracking. Custom storefronts use tracking code on their homepage and thank-you page, with product fulfilment handled separately. Wrapping Things Up

The shape of influencer marketing has changed a lot in the past few years. Brand planning to incorporate influencer campaigns in their future marketing strategies must evolve to adopt these changes. 

Some of them include: 

  • Working with micro-influencers. Celebrities and mega-influencers don’t do it for every business. If you need someone with an engaged audience in your particular niche, a micro or nano-influencer is your best bet. 
  • Working with multiple influencers. Don’t settle with one or two influencers. Collaborate with a few to ensure you’re reaching most, if not all, sub-segments of your audience. 
  • Analyzing results. Influencer marketing can often take time to take off. Don't give up too quickly. Keep track of your campaigns and analyze the results regularly. 
  • Measuring different metrics. Not all brands use ROI as the main metric. Some opt for impressions, while others focus on brand mentions. Go through our report to learn more about this trend.
