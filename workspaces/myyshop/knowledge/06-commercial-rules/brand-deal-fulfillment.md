# Brand Deal Fulfillment

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/06-commercial-rules/brand-deal-fulfillment.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

When is a brand collaboration considered fulfilled?

## 2. Current answer

Brand Deals separates content creation, product promotion and affiliate promotion. Content creation can involve delivering an asset without publishing it; product promotion involves social distribution and approved submissions; affiliate promotion links compensation to sales. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-brand-deals-001.md)

## 3. Details and conditions

- The agreement requires submitted content to meet disclosed platform and advertiser standards. Section 3.6 describes revisions and different completion rules: content-production orders can complete on approval, while social-promotion orders also require posting and retaining content for more than 30 days as specified. Confirm which agreement and campaign rules apply before reusing this condition. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md)
- Paid for Every View is a TikTok video campaign model accessed through Opportunities. Earnings depend on eligible views of approved videos during the campaign’s measurement period, subject to the advertised rate, remaining budget, deadline and payout limits. [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md) [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-imh-16.md)
- The Creator Service Agreement describes determining the validity of completed commercial orders and calculating revenue within 7 days after completion. That clause concerns revenue calculation and account crediting, not a verified universal external payout time for CPM, MyyFinds or every provider. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

Apply the relevant product, campaign, market and current agreement. Keep account registration, campaign approval, payable activity, settlement and external receipt distinct.

## 5. Evidence gaps and review

- **G03**: Confirm offer-specific CPM and collaboration rules: valid activity, rates, budget, deadlines, review, sample format, posting obligations and payout caps.
- **G06**: Confirm settlement, payout methods, external receipt time, taxes, conversion, holds and withdrawal limits by product and market; do not equate the 7-day calculation clause with bank receipt.
- **G07**: Confirm current governing agreements and applicability, including content licence, social-post retention, complaints, cancellation and advertising-disclosure procedures.
