# Supported Creator Platforms

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/06-commercial-rules/supported-platforms.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

Which social platforms are named, and for which purpose?

## 2. Current answer

The About page names TikTok, Instagram and YouTube account connection. The CPM release specifically describes TikTok video campaigns. Other channels named for marketing or link sharing must not automatically be listed as equivalent integrations. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md)

## 3. Details and conditions

- The MyyFinds page describes sharing links through social profiles, posts and messaging channels, including TikTok, Instagram, Twitter, WhatsApp and Facebook groups. A place to share a link is not the same as an officially supported account integration or campaign platform. [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-myyfinds-001.md)
- Paid for Every View is a TikTok video campaign model accessed through Opportunities. Earnings depend on eligible views of approved videos during the campaign’s measurement period, subject to the advertised rate, remaining budget, deadline and payout limits. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md) [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-imh-16.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

Apply the relevant product, campaign, market and current agreement. Keep account registration, campaign approval, payable activity, settlement and external receipt distinct.
