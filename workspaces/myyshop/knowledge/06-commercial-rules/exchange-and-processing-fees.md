# Exchange, Tax and Processing Fees

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/06-commercial-rules/exchange-and-processing-fees.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

What is known about taxes, exchange rates and processing charges?

## 2. Current answer

The public platform-fee statements do not resolve tax liability, exchange rates, conversion spreads or payment-provider charges. Those costs and withholding arrangements require the current payout terms for the creator’s market and method.

## 3. Details and conditions

- The About page says creators pay no platform commission on earnings generated through the platform, and the homepage says there are no platform withdrawal fees. Keep those fee categories separate from taxes, currency conversion, payment-provider charges and separately priced services. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-homepage-001.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-report.md)
- The homepage, About and Brand Deals pages describe requesting a withdrawal once the balance reaches $50. The Creator Service Agreement says the withdrawal interface determines applicable withdrawal standards. Reaching the threshold is not a promise of immediate bank or wallet receipt. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-homepage-001.md) [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-brand-deals-001.md) [S5](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

Apply the relevant product, campaign, market and current agreement. Keep account registration, campaign approval, payable activity, settlement and external receipt distinct.

## 5. Evidence gaps and review

- **G05**: Confirm the scope of zero platform commission and withdrawal fees, separately priced services and limited-time offers, including Free Value Report and courses.
- **G06**: Confirm settlement, payout methods, external receipt time, taxes, conversion, holds and withdrawal limits by product and market; do not equate the 7-day calculation clause with bank receipt.
