# Content Submission Rules

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/06-commercial-rules/content-submission.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

What must submitted content satisfy?

## 2. Current answer

The agreement requires submitted content to meet disclosed platform and advertiser standards. Section 3.6 describes revisions and different completion rules: content-production orders can complete on approval, while social-promotion orders also require posting and retaining content for more than 30 days as specified. Confirm which agreement and campaign rules apply before reusing this condition. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md)

## 3. Details and conditions

- The Brand Deals page states up to 10 campaign applications per day and up to 2 advertiser-requested revisions. These are published workflow limits for that page, not a promise of acceptance or a universal rule for every campaign type. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-brand-deals-001.md)
- Creators must have the necessary rights and meet the relevant brief and review standards. No-sample campaigns may use authorized brand assets, but permission to use those assets is not a licence for misleading product claims, fabricated experience or stolen content. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md)
- Paid for Every View is a TikTok video campaign model accessed through Opportunities. Earnings depend on eligible views of approved videos during the campaign’s measurement period, subject to the advertised rate, remaining budget, deadline and payout limits. [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md) [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-imh-16.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

Apply the relevant product, campaign, market and current agreement. Keep account registration, campaign approval, payable activity, settlement and external receipt distinct.

## 5. Evidence gaps and review

- **G03**: Confirm offer-specific CPM and collaboration rules: valid activity, rates, budget, deadlines, review, sample format, posting obligations and payout caps.
- **G07**: Confirm current governing agreements and applicability, including content licence, social-post retention, complaints, cancellation and advertising-disclosure procedures.
