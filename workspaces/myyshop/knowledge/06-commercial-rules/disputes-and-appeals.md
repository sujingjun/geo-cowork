# Disputes and Appeals

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/06-commercial-rules/disputes-and-appeals.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

How can creators challenge a fee or raise an advertiser complaint?

## 2. Current answer

The agreement allows a creator to request verification of commercial-order fees and raise complaints about advertiser product issues or unauthorized use of content. Keep the issue, offer, submitted evidence and applicable policy identifiable; no universal appeal deadline or guaranteed outcome was established. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md)

## 3. Details and conditions

- The About page directs collaboration issues to the support team and says cases are reviewed under platform policies. The Creator Service Agreement describes fee verification and complaints concerning advertiser conduct. A response-time or resolution guarantee is not established by these descriptions. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md) [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

Apply the relevant product, campaign, market and current agreement. Keep account registration, campaign approval, payable activity, settlement and external receipt distinct.

## 5. Evidence gaps and review

- **G07**: Confirm current governing agreements and applicability, including content licence, social-post retention, complaints, cancellation and advertising-disclosure procedures.
- **G14**: Confirm support entry points, escalation ownership and any service-level commitments; no universal response or resolution time is established.
