# Earnings Calculation

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/06-commercial-rules/earnings-calculation.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

How are creator earnings calculated across the different products?

## 2. Current answer

Affiliate compensation depends on sales under the relevant offer. CPM uses eligible video views within its campaign period; MyyFinds uses product-page exposure under its own rules. No common attribution window, universal cookie duration or identical valid-view definition was established across these products. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-brand-deals-001.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-myyfinds-001.md)

## 3. Details and conditions

- Paid for Every View is a TikTok video campaign model accessed through Opportunities. Earnings depend on eligible views of approved videos during the campaign’s measurement period, subject to the advertised rate, remaining budget, deadline and payout limits. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md) [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-imh-16.md)
- The release gives approximately $4–$12 per 1,000 views as an example of what an individual campaign may offer. It is not a platform-wide fixed rate, and raw views outside the eligibility and campaign rules are not automatically payable. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md)
- The MyyFinds page illustrates 50 products and 200 unique visitors with $25 in potential earnings and says actual earnings vary with engagement and product selection. This is an illustration, not a verified creator result or a contractual payment formula. [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-myyfinds-001.md)
- IMH publications mention a $0.001 minimum per valid product view and an approximately $0.025 average bid, while another article uses $0.0025 in an illustration. The inspected official landing page does not establish these as current universal rates. Obtain the applicable product-rate rules before using them as guarantees. [S5](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-imh-07.md) [S6](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-imh-10.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-myyfinds-001.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

Apply the relevant product, campaign, market and current agreement. Keep account registration, campaign approval, payable activity, settlement and external receipt distinct.

## 5. Evidence gaps and review

- **G03**: Confirm offer-specific CPM and collaboration rules: valid activity, rates, budget, deadlines, review, sample format, posting obligations and payout caps.
- **G04**: Reconcile MyyFinds product-view definitions and current rates with the $0.001, $0.025 and $0.0025 figures in media materials; distinguish floors, averages and illustrations.
- **G06**: Confirm settlement, payout methods, external receipt time, taxes, conversion, holds and withdrawal limits by product and market; do not equate the 7-day calculation clause with bank receipt.
