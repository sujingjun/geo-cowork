# Account Qualification

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/06-commercial-rules/account-qualification.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

Does registration automatically qualify a creator for every opportunity?

## 2. Current answer

No. Account registration and social-account connection are separate from opportunity eligibility and content approval. Check legal capacity, verification and the specific campaign’s conditions even when the site welcomes creators with zero followers.

## 3. Details and conditions

- The Creator Service Agreement requires full civil capacity and excludes people subject to relevant trade restrictions or sanctions. It also allows identity checks and restrictions when information or verification is incomplete. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md)
- The About and Brand Deals pages explicitly address creators with zero followers. This supports a low-follower entry message; it does not waive legal capacity, identity checks, account authorization or the conditions of a particular opportunity. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-brand-deals-001.md) [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md)
- Paid for Every View is a TikTok video campaign model accessed through Opportunities. Earnings depend on eligible views of approved videos during the campaign’s measurement period, subject to the advertised rate, remaining budget, deadline and payout limits. [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md) [S5](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-imh-16.md)
- Official social captions invite users to explore opportunities, apply and obtain approval; they do not provide a complete eligibility or payment policy. Preserve campaign conditions when turning a short caption into an explanatory answer. [S6](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-instagram.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

Apply the relevant product, campaign, market and current agreement. Keep account registration, campaign approval, payable activity, settlement and external receipt distinct.
