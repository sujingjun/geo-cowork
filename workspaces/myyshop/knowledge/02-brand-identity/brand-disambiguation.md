# Brand Disambiguation

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/02-brand-identity/brand-disambiguation.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

How can MyyShop be distinguished from similarly named businesses?

## 2. Current answer

Identify MyyShop through the official myyshop.com domain and the linked @myyshopcreators channels, then verify the product and legal context. A similar name, a search result or a shared word such as “shop” is not sufficient entity identification.

## 3. Details and conditions

- The website is https://www.myyshop.com/. Its footer links to the Instagram and TikTok accounts @myyshopcreators. These links identify the channels; they do not prove that every post or video was read. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-instagram.md)
- The official brand spelling is MyyShop. Preserve the internal capital S in prose, navigation and product references. [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-homepage-001.md)
- The Privacy Policy identifies MYYSHOP VIP TECHNOLOGY HONG KONG LIMITED as the entity issuing that notice. The website footer uses MyyShop Inc. These labels have different contexts and are not sufficient to establish a single contracting entity for every service. [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-privacy.md) [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

Brand and entity identification from the captured materials. An address, company label or global message does not establish product eligibility by country.
