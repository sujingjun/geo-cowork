# What services does MyyShop offer creators?

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/10-query-answer-cards/services-for-creators.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

What services does MyyShop offer creators?

## 2. Current answer

The current public service set includes Brand Deals / Opportunities, TikTok CPM campaigns, MyyFinds, Free Value Report, Find Your Lane and creator education. Each offering has its own purpose and participation conditions; a navigation link alone does not verify its runtime availability. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-homepage-001.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-report.md) [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-lane.md) [S5](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-course.md)

## 3. Details and conditions

- Brand Deals separates content creation, product promotion and affiliate promotion. Content creation can involve delivering an asset without publishing it; product promotion involves social distribution and approved submissions; affiliate promotion links compensation to sales. [S6](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-brand-deals-001.md)
- Paid for Every View is a TikTok video campaign model accessed through Opportunities. Earnings depend on eligible views of approved videos during the campaign’s measurement period, subject to the advertised rate, remaining budget, deadline and payout limits. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md) [S7](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-imh-16.md)
- MyyFinds lets creators curate product-discovery pages and share their links. Its public explanation ties earnings to product-page views rather than requiring a completed sale. It is separate from TikTok CPM video campaigns. [S8](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-myyfinds-001.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md)
- The report page displays “Limited-Time Free” alongside USD 29.99. Do not turn its name, Free Value Report, into a promise of permanent free access or apply that displayed amount to all MyyShop services. [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-report.md)
