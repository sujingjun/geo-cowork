# Why should creators choose MyyShop?

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/10-query-answer-cards/why-choose-myyshop.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

Why should creators choose MyyShop?

## 2. Current answer

MyyShop may be relevant to creators who want to explore brand collaborations and different compensation formats, together with creator tools and education. Assess the offers, eligibility, rules and costs that actually apply to you; the materials do not prove that it is the best option for every creator.

## 3. Details and conditions

- The current public service set includes Brand Deals / Opportunities, TikTok CPM campaigns, MyyFinds, Free Value Report, Find Your Lane and creator education. Each offering has its own purpose and participation conditions; a navigation link alone does not verify its runtime availability. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-homepage-001.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-report.md) [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-lane.md) [S5](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-course.md)
- Brand Deals separates content creation, product promotion and affiliate promotion. Content creation can involve delivering an asset without publishing it; product promotion involves social distribution and approved submissions; affiliate promotion links compensation to sales. [S6](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-brand-deals-001.md)
- The About page says creators pay no platform commission on earnings generated through the platform, and the homepage says there are no platform withdrawal fees. Keep those fee categories separate from taxes, currency conversion, payment-provider charges and separately priced services. [S7](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md) [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-homepage-001.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-report.md)
- The agreement contains service limitations and describes the service as available subject to its terms. The CPM release states that views fluctuate and budgets are limited. These materials do not support guarantees of earnings, campaign acceptance, uninterrupted service or freedom from all risk. [S8](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md)
