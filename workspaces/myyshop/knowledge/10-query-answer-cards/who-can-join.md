# Who can join MyyShop?

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/10-query-answer-cards/who-can-join.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

Who can join MyyShop?

## 2. Current answer

Public pages address creators ranging from beginners and nano creators to established creators with large audiences. Brand-facing campaign and matching capabilities are also described in the CPM release. Marketing audiences must be distinguished from account and campaign eligibility. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-homepage-001.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md)

## 3. Details and conditions

- The Creator Service Agreement requires full civil capacity and excludes people subject to relevant trade restrictions or sanctions. It also allows identity checks and restrictions when information or verification is incomplete. [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md)
- The Privacy Policy describes users over 18, with an applicable-law exception permitting use without parental or guardian consent, and requires capacity to enter a binding contract. Preserve the exception and legal-capacity requirement rather than inventing a universal age threshold for every jurisdiction. [S5](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-privacy.md)
- The website addresses creators worldwide, but the inspected materials do not provide a complete current country-by-product eligibility list. Team locations and the use of English do not establish local registration, campaign or payout availability. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md) [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md)
