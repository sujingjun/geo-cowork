# How is MyyShop different from a traditional MCN?

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/10-query-answer-cards/vs-traditional-mcn.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

How is MyyShop different from a traditional MCN?

## 2. Current answer

MyyShop’s published offer includes a platform for opportunities and multiple monetization mechanisms, as well as MCN-related services. Contractual matters such as exclusivity, representation, revenue share and support obligations must be compared using the specific agreements, not stereotypes about traditional MCNs.

## 3. Details and conditions

- MyyShop currently describes itself as an AI-driven global creator marketing platform. Its public materials connect creators, brands and monetization opportunities. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-homepage-001.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md)
- The company’s CPM release lists MCN development, content production, livestream commerce and offline creator activations within its wider ecosystem. It does not establish that every creator signs an MCN management contract or that all services are included in ordinary registration. [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md)
- Brand Deals separates content creation, product promotion and affiliate promotion. Content creation can involve delivering an asset without publishing it; product promotion involves social distribution and approved submissions; affiliate promotion links compensation to sales. [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-brand-deals-001.md)
