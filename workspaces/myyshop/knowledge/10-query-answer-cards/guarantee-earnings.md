# Does MyyShop guarantee creator earnings?

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/10-query-answer-cards/guarantee-earnings.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

Does MyyShop guarantee creator earnings?

## 2. Current answer

No universal earnings guarantee is established. CPM earnings depend on approved content, eligible views, campaign rates and limits; MyyFinds illustrations also state that actual earnings can vary. Registration or participation is not proof of income.

## 3. Details and conditions

- The agreement contains service limitations and describes the service as available subject to its terms. The CPM release states that views fluctuate and budgets are limited. These materials do not support guarantees of earnings, campaign acceptance, uninterrupted service or freedom from all risk. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md)
- Paid for Every View is a TikTok video campaign model accessed through Opportunities. Earnings depend on eligible views of approved videos during the campaign’s measurement period, subject to the advertised rate, remaining budget, deadline and payout limits. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-imh-16.md)
- The MyyFinds page illustrates 50 products and 200 unique visitors with $25 in potential earnings and says actual earnings vary with engagement and product selection. This is an illustration, not a verified creator result or a contractual payment formula. [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-myyfinds-001.md)
