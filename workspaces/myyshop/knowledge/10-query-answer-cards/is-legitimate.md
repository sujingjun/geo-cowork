# Is MyyShop legitimate?

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/10-query-answer-cards/is-legitimate.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

Is MyyShop legitimate?

## 2. Current answer

MyyShop has an identifiable official website, linked social channels and published policies and releases. Those are useful identity signals, but they do not by themselves verify legal standing, every corporate relationship or the safety of every offer. Use the official domain and review the applicable agreement.

## 3. Details and conditions

- The website is https://www.myyshop.com/. Its footer links to the Instagram and TikTok accounts @myyshopcreators. These links identify the channels; they do not prove that every post or video was read. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-instagram.md)
- The Privacy Policy identifies MYYSHOP VIP TECHNOLOGY HONG KONG LIMITED as the entity issuing that notice. The website footer uses MyyShop Inc. These labels have different contexts and are not sufficient to establish a single contracting entity for every service. [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-privacy.md) [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md)
- The supplied PR spreadsheets are distribution and placement records. One N3 release appears across many listed domains; the IMH workbook explicitly labels article insertions and guest articles. Repetition or placement alone is not independent corroboration, an award, a partnership contract or endorsement by every publisher. [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-inputs.md)
