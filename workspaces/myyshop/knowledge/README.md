English is the primary version. The Simplified Chinese files are complete reading counterparts. There are 120 logical entries: 94 source-fact entries and 26 derived answers, all drafts for internal review.

## How to use this edition

- Start with the current answer, then read its conditions, evidence and gaps. Every entry links to its counterpart and supporting knowledge.
- Current public positioning is the baseline. Whether brand positioning will change is undecided; this edition does not claim a completed or ongoing lifecycle-platform transformation.
- Editorial knowledge questions organize the material. They are not demand-frequency observations, formal Queries or a frozen Query Set.
- Website statements, published PR and official social captions are attributed evidence of what was said. Product delivery, current applicability and business approval are separate checks.
- Original material is retained in evidence snapshots. Existing canonical documents, claims, releases and historical decisions remain unchanged and are not replaced by these drafts.
- canonical_target, where present, points to an inspected public reference page. It is not a verified HTML canonical tag or proof that a proposed page was published.
