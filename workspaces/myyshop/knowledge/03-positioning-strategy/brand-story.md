# Brand Story

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/03-positioning-strategy/brand-story.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

What brand story can be told without inventing a positioning change?

## 2. Current answer

The current public story connects creator monetization and brand collaboration with commerce infrastructure described as backed by DHgate Group. Keep historical descriptions in their original time context and do not frame this as a confirmed lifecycle-platform transformation.

## 3. Details and conditions

- MyyShop currently describes itself as an AI-driven global creator marketing platform. Its public materials connect creators, brands and monetization opportunities. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-homepage-001.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md)
- The MyyShop-issued CPM press release describes MyyShop as backed by DHgate Group. That is a published relationship statement, not a corporate ownership chart or a guarantee by another group company. [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md)
- The About page frames MyyShop’s mission around helping creators monetize their influence and turn creativity into sustainable income. This is the company’s stated ambition, not a promise of an individual creator’s earnings. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md)
- For this initial knowledge edition, use the current public positioning. A change of brand positioning has not been decided; do not state that a lifecycle-platform transformation is completed or underway. [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-direction.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

Current public narrative and the user’s direction for this edition. Historical statements remain dated context; a positioning transformation is not assumed.
