# Company Background

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/03-positioning-strategy/company-background.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

What company background is supported by the current materials?

## 2. Current answer

The MyyShop-issued CPM press release describes MyyShop as backed by DHgate Group. That is a published relationship statement, not a corporate ownership chart or a guarantee by another group company. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md)

## 3. Details and conditions

- The website publishes a Los Angeles contact address. The CPM press release describes teams in Los Angeles, Shenzhen and Beijing. An address or team location does not establish a registered office, headquarters designation or service eligibility by country. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md) [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md)
- The Privacy Policy identifies MYYSHOP VIP TECHNOLOGY HONG KONG LIMITED as the entity issuing that notice. The website footer uses MyyShop Inc. These labels have different contexts and are not sufficient to establish a single contracting entity for every service. [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-privacy.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

Current public narrative and the user’s direction for this edition. Historical statements remain dated context; a positioning transformation is not assumed.

## 5. Evidence gaps and review

- **G01**: Confirm the legal entity for each service, its relationship to the privacy-notice entity and footer name, and the role of each published address.
- **G11**: Clarify company and product chronology, service packages, MCN arrangements and the scope of named collaborations or credentials.
