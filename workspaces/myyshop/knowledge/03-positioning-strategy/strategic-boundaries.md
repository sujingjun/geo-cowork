Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

What strategic boundaries apply to this initial knowledge edition?

## 2. Current answer

For this initial knowledge edition, use the current public positioning. A change of brand positioning has not been decided; do not state that a lifecycle-platform transformation is completed or underway. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-direction.md)

## 3. Details and conditions

- The current public service set includes Brand Deals / Opportunities, TikTok CPM campaigns, MyyFinds, Free Value Report, Find Your Lane and creator education. Each offering has its own purpose and participation conditions; a navigation link alone does not verify its runtime availability. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-homepage-001.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md) [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-report.md) [S5](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-lane.md) [S6](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-course.md)
- The first GEO research group is English-speaking creators in the US who seek their first monetization opportunity. This is a research and prioritization setting, not proof that every member of that group is eligible for a MyyShop service or payment method. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-direction.md)
- The agreement contains service limitations and describes the service as available subject to its terms. The CPM release states that views fluctuate and budgets are limited. These materials do not support guarantees of earnings, campaign acceptance, uninterrupted service or freedom from all risk. [S7](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md)

Current public narrative and the user’s direction for this edition. Historical statements remain dated context; a positioning transformation is not assumed.
