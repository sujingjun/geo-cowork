# Core Beliefs

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/03-positioning-strategy/core-beliefs.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

Which core beliefs can be attributed to the current public narrative?

## 2. Current answer

The About page frames MyyShop’s mission around helping creators monetize their influence and turn creativity into sustainable income. This is the company’s stated ambition, not a promise of an individual creator’s earnings. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md)

## 3. Details and conditions

- The official Instagram uses direct invitations, creator-friendly language and encouragement to explore ideas and collaborations. These are observed channel patterns, not a formally approved universal brand-personality or tone handbook. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-instagram.md)
- The agreement contains service limitations and describes the service as available subject to its terms. The CPM release states that views fluctuate and budgets are limited. These materials do not support guarantees of earnings, campaign acceptance, uninterrupted service or freedom from all risk. [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md) [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

Current public narrative and the user’s direction for this edition. Historical statements remain dated context; a positioning transformation is not assumed.
