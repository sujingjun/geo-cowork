Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

What vision statement is available for MyyShop?

## 2. Current answer

The About page discusses an open, intelligent creator ecosystem and opportunities for creators worldwide. The inspected materials do not provide a separately approved vision statement or a complete formal values charter. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md)

## 3. Details and conditions

- The About page frames MyyShop’s mission around helping creators monetize their influence and turn creativity into sustainable income. This is the company’s stated ambition, not a promise of an individual creator’s earnings. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md)
- For this initial knowledge edition, use the current public positioning. A change of brand positioning has not been decided; do not state that a lifecycle-platform transformation is completed or underway. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-direction.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

Current public narrative and the user’s direction for this edition. Historical statements remain dated context; a positioning transformation is not assumed.
