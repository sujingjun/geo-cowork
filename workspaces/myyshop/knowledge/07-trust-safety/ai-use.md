# AI Use and Explainability

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/07-trust-safety/ai-use.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

What AI use is described, and what can a creator ask about it?

## 2. Current answer

The Privacy Policy describes manual or automated opportunity recommendations using account and activity information. It also describes rights to seek an explanation and object to solely automated decisions, including possible human review. The actual request workflow and response performance were not tested. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-privacy.md)

## 3. Details and conditions

- Free Value Report is presented as an AI-assisted commercial-value analysis offering: niche positioning, audience profile, persona assessment, peer benchmarking and potential brand opportunities. The inspected page is a product description; no live report was generated in this task. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-report.md)
- The About page and CPM release publish figures of 33M+ SKUs and 1.66M creators in an AI/matching context. They do not supply a dated, reproducible count of active creators, registered accounts, available products or currently open campaigns. [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md) [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

This summarizes the published policy and its limits. Policy wording, actual operations and jurisdiction-specific applicability require separate verification.

## 5. Evidence gaps and review

- **G17**: Confirm AI capability, data provenance, model/measurement claims and explanation or human-review procedures before making stronger technical or effectiveness statements.
- **G09**: Obtain definitions, populations, dates, baselines and reproducible records for scale, activity, efficiency, accuracy, creator-growth and income claims.
