# Content and Intellectual Property Rights

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/07-trust-safety/content-rights.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

Who owns creator content, and what use rights are described?

## 2. Current answer

The agreement states that creator-supplied content belongs to the creator or relevant rights holders, while section 8.3 also describes a broad worldwide exclusive licence and sublicensing rights. Ownership and licensed use are different; the shorter About-page copyright statement must not erase the agreement’s permissions. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md)

## 3. Details and conditions

- Creators must have the necessary rights and meet the relevant brief and review standards. No-sample campaigns may use authorized brand assets, but permission to use those assets is not a licence for misleading product claims, fabricated experience or stolen content. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md)
- The agreement allows a creator to request verification of commercial-order fees and raise complaints about advertiser product issues or unauthorized use of content. Keep the issue, offer, submitted evidence and applicable policy identifiable; no universal appeal deadline or guaranteed outcome was established. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md)

This summarizes the published policy and its limits. Policy wording, actual operations and jurisdiction-specific applicability require separate verification.
