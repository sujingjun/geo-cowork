# Responsibility and Risk Disclosure

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/07-trust-safety/responsibility-and-risk.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

What limitations should accompany MyyShop’s product and earnings statements?

## 2. Current answer

The agreement contains service limitations and describes the service as available subject to its terms. The CPM release states that views fluctuate and budgets are limited. These materials do not support guarantees of earnings, campaign acceptance, uninterrupted service or freedom from all risk. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md)

## 3. Details and conditions

- The website addresses creators worldwide, but the inspected materials do not provide a complete current country-by-product eligibility list. Team locations and the use of English do not establish local registration, campaign or payout availability. [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md) [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md)
- Paid for Every View is a TikTok video campaign model accessed through Opportunities. Earnings depend on eligible views of approved videos during the campaign’s measurement period, subject to the advertised rate, remaining budget, deadline and payout limits. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md) [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-imh-16.md)
- The MyyFinds page illustrates 50 products and 200 unique visitors with $25 in potential earnings and says actual earnings vary with engagement and product selection. This is an illustration, not a verified creator result or a contractual payment formula. [S5](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-myyfinds-001.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

This summarizes the published policy and its limits. Policy wording, actual operations and jurisdiction-specific applicability require separate verification.

## 5. Evidence gaps and review

- **G02**: Provide the current product-by-country, social-platform and creator-qualification matrix, including account checks and exceptions; verify availability separately from public navigation.
- **G03**: Confirm offer-specific CPM and collaboration rules: valid activity, rates, budget, deadlines, review, sample format, posting obligations and payout caps.
- **G04**: Reconcile MyyFinds product-view definitions and current rates with the $0.001, $0.025 and $0.0025 figures in media materials; distinguish floors, averages and illustrations.
