# Minor Protection

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/07-trust-safety/minor-protection.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

What do the public materials say about minors?

## 2. Current answer

The Privacy Policy describes users over 18, with an applicable-law exception permitting use without parental or guardian consent, and requires capacity to enter a binding contract. Preserve the exception and legal-capacity requirement rather than inventing a universal age threshold for every jurisdiction. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-privacy.md)

## 3. Details and conditions

- The Creator Service Agreement requires full civil capacity and excludes people subject to relevant trade restrictions or sanctions. It also allows identity checks and restrictions when information or verification is incomplete. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md)
- The Privacy Policy displays an update and effective date of 2025-06-05. It distinguishes website visitors, creators, organizations and other processing contexts. Its publication is evidence of stated policy, not an audit of every operational practice. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-privacy.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

This summarizes the published policy and its limits. Policy wording, actual operations and jurisdiction-specific applicability require separate verification.

## 5. Evidence gaps and review

- **G02**: Provide the current product-by-country, social-platform and creator-qualification matrix, including account checks and exceptions; verify availability separately from public navigation.
- **G08**: Verify the operational consent, access, deletion, account-cancellation and retention routes described by the Privacy Policy, without inventing response times or universal data-location promises.
