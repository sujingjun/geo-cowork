# Advertising Disclosure

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/07-trust-safety/ad-disclosure.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

How should advertising or paid-collaboration disclosure be handled?

## 2. Current answer

Campaign briefs may require hashtags and creative instructions. The material set does not provide a complete market-by-market advertising-disclosure procedure. Campaign hashtags and a platform connection do not by themselves prove compliant sponsorship disclosure. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md)

## 3. Details and conditions

- Creators must have the necessary rights and meet the relevant brief and review standards. No-sample campaigns may use authorized brand assets, but permission to use those assets is not a licence for misleading product claims, fabricated experience or stolen content. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md) [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md)

This summarizes the published policy and its limits. Policy wording, actual operations and jurisdiction-specific applicability require separate verification.
