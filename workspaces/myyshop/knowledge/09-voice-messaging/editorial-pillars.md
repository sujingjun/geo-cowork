# Editorial Pillars

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/09-voice-messaging/editorial-pillars.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

Which editorial themes are supported by current materials?

## 2. Current answer

Useful observed themes are brand collaboration, creator monetization, content skills, opportunity discovery and product-rule clarity. They organize this draft; they are not evidence that a new brand strategy or a lifecycle transformation has been adopted.

## 3. Details and conditions

- The current public service set includes Brand Deals / Opportunities, TikTok CPM campaigns, MyyFinds, Free Value Report, Find Your Lane and creator education. Each offering has its own purpose and participation conditions; a navigation link alone does not verify its runtime availability. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-homepage-001.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-report.md) [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-lane.md) [S5](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-course.md)
- Public growth-oriented resources include account analysis, peer examples, creator education and feedback on submitted content. These describe support mechanisms, not a guaranteed follower increase, growth rate or business result. [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-report.md) [S5](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-course.md) [S6](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-brand-deals-001.md)
- The official Instagram uses direct invitations, creator-friendly language and encouragement to explore ideas and collaborations. These are observed channel patterns, not a formally approved universal brand-personality or tone handbook. [S7](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-instagram.md)
- For this initial knowledge edition, use the current public positioning. A change of brand positioning has not been decided; do not state that a lifecycle-platform transformation is completed or underway. [S8](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-direction.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

Editorial guidance for this initial knowledge edition. Observed channel style is distinguished from a formally adopted brand handbook.
