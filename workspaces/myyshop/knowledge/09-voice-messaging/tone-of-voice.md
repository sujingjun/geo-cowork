# Tone of Voice

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/09-voice-messaging/tone-of-voice.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

How should the tone differ between promotion and factual explanation?

## 2. Current answer

Use clear, direct English for knowledge and rules. Social warmth can be preserved in channel examples, while fees, eligibility and rights need explicit conditions. Do not translate promotional enthusiasm into a stronger factual promise.

## 3. Details and conditions

- The official Instagram uses direct invitations, creator-friendly language and encouragement to explore ideas and collaborations. These are observed channel patterns, not a formally approved universal brand-personality or tone handbook. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-instagram.md)
- Keep eligibility, time limits, review requirements, budgets and exceptions next to the claim they qualify. Do not upgrade may or can into will or guaranteed, or turn valid product views into all views. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-direction.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md) [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-myyfinds-001.md)
- The agreement contains service limitations and describes the service as available subject to its terms. The CPM release states that views fluctuate and budgets are limited. These materials do not support guarantees of earnings, campaign acceptance, uninterrupted service or freedom from all risk. [S5](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

Editorial guidance for this initial knowledge edition. Observed channel style is distinguished from a formally adopted brand handbook.
