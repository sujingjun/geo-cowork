# Brand Slogan

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/09-voice-messaging/brand-slogan.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

Which slogans or taglines are currently visible?

## 2. Current answer

The materials use several campaign and page messages around creativity, collaboration and monetization. Keep a slogan with its page or campaign context; a single universal brand slogan and retirement list have not been established for this edition.

## 3. Details and conditions

- The About page frames MyyShop’s mission around helping creators monetize their influence and turn creativity into sustainable income. This is the company’s stated ambition, not a promise of an individual creator’s earnings. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md)
- The official Instagram uses direct invitations, creator-friendly language and encouragement to explore ideas and collaborations. These are observed channel patterns, not a formally approved universal brand-personality or tone handbook. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-instagram.md)
- For this initial knowledge edition, use the current public positioning. A change of brand positioning has not been decided; do not state that a lifecycle-platform transformation is completed or underway. [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-direction.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

Editorial guidance for this initial knowledge edition. Observed channel style is distinguished from a formally adopted brand handbook.

## 5. Evidence gaps and review
