# Terminology

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/09-voice-messaging/terminology.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

Which terms should stay consistent across the two languages?

## 2. Current answer

Use MyyShop for the platform; Brand Deals / Opportunities for campaign discovery; Paid for Every View / TikTok CPM for approved-video view-based campaigns; MyyFinds for product-discovery exposure; and Free Value Report / Find Your Lane for their respective described services. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-homepage-001.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-lane.md) [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-report.md)

## 3. Details and conditions

- The official brand spelling is MyyShop. Preserve the internal capital S in prose, navigation and product references. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-homepage-001.md)
- Keep eligibility, time limits, review requirements, budgets and exceptions next to the claim they qualify. Do not upgrade may or can into will or guaranteed, or turn valid product views into all views. [S5](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-direction.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md) [S6](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-myyfinds-001.md)
