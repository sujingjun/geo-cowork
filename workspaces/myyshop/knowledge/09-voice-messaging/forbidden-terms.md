# Forbidden Terms and High-risk Claims

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/09-voice-messaging/forbidden-terms.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

Which wording would exceed the evidence?

## 2. Current answer

Keep eligibility, time limits, review requirements, budgets and exceptions next to the claim they qualify. Do not upgrade may or can into will or guaranteed, or turn valid product views into all views. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-direction.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-myyfinds-001.md)

## 3. Details and conditions

- The agreement contains service limitations and describes the service as available subject to its terms. The CPM release states that views fluctuate and budgets are limited. These materials do not support guarantees of earnings, campaign acceptance, uninterrupted service or freedom from all risk. [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md)
- The report page displays “Limited-Time Free” alongside USD 29.99. Do not turn its name, Free Value Report, into a promise of permanent free access or apply that displayed amount to all MyyShop services. [S5](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-report.md)
- IMH publications mention a $0.001 minimum per valid product view and an approximately $0.025 average bid, while another article uses $0.0025 in an illustration. The inspected official landing page does not establish these as current universal rates. Obtain the applicable product-rate rules before using them as guarantees. [S6](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-imh-07.md) [S7](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-imh-10.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-myyfinds-001.md)
