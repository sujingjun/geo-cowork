# Creator Support

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/05-products-services/creator-support.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

Where should a creator raise collaboration or payment problems?

## 2. Current answer

The About page directs collaboration issues to the support team and says cases are reviewed under platform policies. The Creator Service Agreement describes fee verification and complaints concerning advertiser conduct. A response-time or resolution guarantee is not established by these descriptions. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md)

## 3. Details and conditions

- The agreement allows a creator to request verification of commercial-order fees and raise complaints about advertiser product issues or unauthorized use of content. Keep the issue, offer, submitted evidence and applicable policy identifiable; no universal appeal deadline or guaranteed outcome was established. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md)
- The policy describes access, correction, erasure, objection and consent-withdrawal routes subject to applicable conditions and identity checks. Withdrawing consent does not make all other processing grounds or retention duties disappear. [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-privacy.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

The named service and its described function only. Current public descriptions were read; live product delivery, all markets and every account’s access were not tested.

## 5. Evidence gaps and review

- **G14**: Confirm support entry points, escalation ownership and any service-level commitments; no universal response or resolution time is established.
- **G07**: Confirm current governing agreements and applicability, including content licence, social-post retention, complaints, cancellation and advertising-disclosure procedures.
