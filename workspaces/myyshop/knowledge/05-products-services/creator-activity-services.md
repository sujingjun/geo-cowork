# Creator Activity Services

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/05-products-services/creator-activity-services.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

How does MyyShop encourage ongoing creator participation?

## 2. Current answer

Public materials mention campaigns, challenges and referral rewards as ways to participate. The Instagram account promotes opportunities and creator ideas. Neither post frequency nor promotional language proves a guaranteed supply of opportunities or an activity-based entitlement. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-myyfinds-001.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-instagram.md)

## 3. Details and conditions

- Official social captions invite users to explore opportunities, apply and obtain approval; they do not provide a complete eligibility or payment policy. Preserve campaign conditions when turning a short caption into an explanatory answer. [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-instagram.md)
- The agreement contains service limitations and describes the service as available subject to its terms. The CPM release states that views fluctuate and budgets are limited. These materials do not support guarantees of earnings, campaign acceptance, uninterrupted service or freedom from all risk. [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md) [S5](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

The named service and its described function only. Current public descriptions were read; live product delivery, all markets and every account’s access were not tested.

## 5. Evidence gaps and review

- **G03**: Confirm offer-specific CPM and collaboration rules: valid activity, rates, budget, deadlines, review, sample format, posting obligations and payout caps.
