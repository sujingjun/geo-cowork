# Brand Deals

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/05-products-services/brand-deals.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

What kinds of Brand Deals can creators explore?

## 2. Current answer

Brand Deals separates content creation, product promotion and affiliate promotion. Content creation can involve delivering an asset without publishing it; product promotion involves social distribution and approved submissions; affiliate promotion links compensation to sales. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-brand-deals-001.md)

## 3. Details and conditions

- The Brand Deals page states up to 10 campaign applications per day and up to 2 advertiser-requested revisions. These are published workflow limits for that page, not a promise of acceptance or a universal rule for every campaign type. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-brand-deals-001.md)
- Paid for Every View is a TikTok video campaign model accessed through Opportunities. Earnings depend on eligible views of approved videos during the campaign’s measurement period, subject to the advertised rate, remaining budget, deadline and payout limits. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-imh-16.md)
- The official footer limits free samples to promotion orders involving original content. Separately, selected CPM campaigns can use supplied digital assets without physical samples. These are different formats and neither implies that every user receives free products. [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

The named service and its described function only. Current public descriptions were read; live product delivery, all markets and every account’s access were not tested.
