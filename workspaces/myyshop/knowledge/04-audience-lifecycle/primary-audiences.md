# Primary Audiences

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/04-audience-lifecycle/primary-audiences.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

Who do the current public materials address?

## 2. Current answer

Public pages address creators ranging from beginners and nano creators to established creators with large audiences. Brand-facing campaign and matching capabilities are also described in the CPM release. Marketing audiences must be distinguished from account and campaign eligibility. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-homepage-001.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md)

## 3. Details and conditions

- The first GEO research group is English-speaking creators in the US who seek their first monetization opportunity. This is a research and prioritization setting, not proof that every member of that group is eligible for a MyyShop service or payment method. [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-direction.md)
- The Creator Service Agreement requires full civil capacity and excludes people subject to relevant trade restrictions or sanctions. It also allows identity checks and restrictions when information or verification is incomplete. [S5](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

The audience or practical workflow described here is not a universal eligibility rule, approved segmentation model or guarantee of outcomes.
