# Onboarding and Awareness Stage

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/04-audience-lifecycle/onboarding-stage.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

What does the onboarding and awareness stage involve?

## 2. Current answer

The homepage describes signing up with an email address or phone number, verifying email, and connecting social accounts to view offers. It then describes adding an address before applying for opportunities. Completing registration is separate from a campaign application or approval. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-homepage-001.md)

## 3. Details and conditions

- The current public service set includes Brand Deals / Opportunities, TikTok CPM campaigns, MyyFinds, Free Value Report, Find Your Lane and creator education. Each offering has its own purpose and participation conditions; a navigation link alone does not verify its runtime availability. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-homepage-001.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-report.md) [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-lane.md) [S5](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-course.md)
- The Creator Service Agreement requires full civil capacity and excludes people subject to relevant trade restrictions or sanctions. It also allows identity checks and restrictions when information or verification is incomplete. [S6](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

The audience or practical workflow described here is not a universal eligibility rule, approved segmentation model or guarantee of outcomes.
