# Cold-start Stage

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/04-audience-lifecycle/cold-start-stage.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

How can a creator explore MyyShop before building a large audience?

## 2. Current answer

The About and Brand Deals pages explicitly address creators with zero followers. This supports a low-follower entry message; it does not waive legal capacity, identity checks, account authorization or the conditions of a particular opportunity. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-brand-deals-001.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md)

## 3. Details and conditions

- The homepage describes signing up with an email address or phone number, verifying email, and connecting social accounts to view offers. It then describes adding an address before applying for opportunities. Completing registration is separate from a campaign application or approval. [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-homepage-001.md)
- Brand Deals separates content creation, product promotion and affiliate promotion. Content creation can involve delivering an asset without publishing it; product promotion involves social distribution and approved submissions; affiliate promotion links compensation to sales. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-brand-deals-001.md)
- The agreement contains service limitations and describes the service as available subject to its terms. The CPM release states that views fluctuate and budgets are limited. These materials do not support guarantees of earnings, campaign acceptance, uninterrupted service or freedom from all risk. [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md) [S5](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

The audience or practical workflow described here is not a universal eligibility rule, approved segmentation model or guarantee of outcomes.
