# Creator Segments

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/04-audience-lifecycle/creator-segments.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

How should creator segments be described?

## 2. Current answer

The site distinguishes beginners, nano creators and established creators in broad terms. Use those descriptive audience groups without inventing follower bands, income tiers or an approved segmentation methodology.

## 3. Details and conditions

- Public pages address creators ranging from beginners and nano creators to established creators with large audiences. Brand-facing campaign and matching capabilities are also described in the CPM release. Marketing audiences must be distinguished from account and campaign eligibility. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-homepage-001.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-globenewswire-cpm-001.md)
- The About and Brand Deals pages explicitly address creators with zero followers. This supports a low-follower entry message; it does not waive legal capacity, identity checks, account authorization or the conditions of a particular opportunity. [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md) [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-brand-deals-001.md) [S5](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

The audience or practical workflow described here is not a universal eligibility rule, approved segmentation model or guarantee of outcomes.

## 5. Evidence gaps and review

- **G13**: A formal creator segmentation or lifecycle model and its thresholds were not supplied. Keep practical workflows separate from a claimed official stage system.
