# Creator Eligibility

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/04-audience-lifecycle/creator-eligibility.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

What qualifications should a prospective creator check?

## 2. Current answer

The About and Brand Deals pages explicitly address creators with zero followers. This supports a low-follower entry message; it does not waive legal capacity, identity checks, account authorization or the conditions of a particular opportunity. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-brand-deals-001.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md)

## 3. Details and conditions

- The Creator Service Agreement requires full civil capacity and excludes people subject to relevant trade restrictions or sanctions. It also allows identity checks and restrictions when information or verification is incomplete. [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md)
- The Privacy Policy describes users over 18, with an applicable-law exception permitting use without parental or guardian consent, and requires capacity to enter a binding contract. Preserve the exception and legal-capacity requirement rather than inventing a universal age threshold for every jurisdiction. [S4](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-privacy.md)
- The website addresses creators worldwide, but the inspected materials do not provide a complete current country-by-product eligibility list. Team locations and the use of English do not establish local registration, campaign or payout availability. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-about-001.md) [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-terms.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

The audience or practical workflow described here is not a universal eligibility rule, approved segmentation model or guarantee of outcomes.
