# Creator Lifecycle Model

[Knowledge home](../README.md) · [中文对照](../translations/zh-CN/04-audience-lifecycle/creator-lifecycle-model.md)

Language: English (primary). Version: 0.2.0. Status: draft for internal review. Source observation date: 2026-10-22.

## 1. Question

Does MyyShop have a confirmed creator lifecycle model?

## 2. Current answer

A practical workflow visible in the public materials is registration, social-account connection, opportunity selection, content submission and earnings review. The materials do not establish an official multi-stage lifecycle model with quantified promotion thresholds. [S1](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-homepage-001.md) [S2](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-web-brand-deals-001.md)

## 3. Details and conditions

- For this initial knowledge edition, use the current public positioning. A change of brand positioning has not been decided; do not state that a lifecycle-platform transformation is completed or underway. [S3](../../evidence/sources/src-myyshop-curation-20261022-direction.md)

## 4. Scope and limits

The audience or practical workflow described here is not a universal eligibility rule, approved segmentation model or guarantee of outcomes.

## 5. Evidence gaps and review

- **G13**: A formal creator segmentation or lifecycle model and its thresholds were not supplied. Keep practical workflows separate from a claimed official stage system.
