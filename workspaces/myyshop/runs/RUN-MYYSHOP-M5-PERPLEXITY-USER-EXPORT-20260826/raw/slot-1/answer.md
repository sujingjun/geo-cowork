## What MyyShop is

**MyyShop** is an AI-powered creator-marketing platform that connects social-media creators and influencers with brands for collaboration opportunities. Creators can use it to find products or campaigns, create promotional content, and potentially earn money through sponsored content or affiliate commissions.[^1_1]

It appears to support creators ranging from small “nano” influencers to established accounts, with opportunities that may include:

- Creating content from a brand brief.
- Promoting products on social media.
- Earning affiliate commissions from sales.
- Receiving products for testing or review.[^1_2]

MyyShop also has an iOS/iPadOS app listed on Apple’s App Store.[^1_3]

As with any influencer platform, review each campaign’s terms carefully—especially payment requirements, product-shipping charges, disclosure obligations, usage rights, and whether you are being asked to pay money upfront.
