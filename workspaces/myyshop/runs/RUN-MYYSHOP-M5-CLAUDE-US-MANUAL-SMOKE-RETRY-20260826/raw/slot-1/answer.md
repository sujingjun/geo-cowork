MyyShop was originally launched by DHgate (a major Chinese cross-border B2B e-commerce marketplace) in 2020 as a SaaS platform for decentralized, cross-border dropshipping. The idea was to let social-media influencers and “private domain traffic” holders (people with followings on Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok, etc.) easily create online stores and offer dropshipping tools, so influencers could monetize their audiences without needing e-commerce expertise, by offering website creation, supplier vetting, AI-based product recommendations, financing, logistics, and after-sales support.

It has since evolved into something broader: today MyyShop describes itself as an AI-driven global creator marketing platform connecting creators with brand collaborations, letting them monetize their content with zero platform fees. It has apps on the App Store and an active presence on TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook, functioning as a bridge between brands and content creators.[1]

In practice, creators can join with AI-powered matching to connect brands with creators globally, and get end-to-end support from content creation through performance tracking and payouts. It is open to creators of any size — even those with zero followers can join, with growth paths unlocking higher-paying campaigns as their following grows.[2][3]

So in short: MyyShop started as a DHgate-backed dropshipping/e-commerce tool and has since repositioned itself primarily as an influencer/creator marketing and brand-collaboration platform.
