MyyShop was launched in 2020 by DHgate as a SaaS product for decentralized cross-border e-commerce, focused on online store creation and dropshipping tools for social commerce influencers.

The platform has evolved since then. Today it is mainly positioned as an AI-driven global creator marketing platform that connects creators with brand collaborations, letting them monetize content with zero platform fees. It supports creators with any number of followers, including zero, across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Reddit, Facebook, and X.

In practice, creators sign up, connect social accounts, and apply to opportunities such as content-creation tasks or product-promotion campaigns.

So in short: MyyShop started as a dropshipping/store-building tool under DHgate and has since rebranded around influencer marketing, matching brands with content creators for paid promotions.
