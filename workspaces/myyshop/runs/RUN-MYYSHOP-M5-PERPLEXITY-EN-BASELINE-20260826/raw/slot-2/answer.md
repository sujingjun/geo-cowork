MyyShop is an AI-driven creator-marketing platform that connects social-media creators with brands for paid collaborations, product promotions, affiliate campaigns, and other ways to monetize an audience.

Creators can typically use MyyShop to find brand collaboration opportunities, create content for product campaigns, promote products and earn commissions, access creator tools such as MyyFinds and free value reports, and work with brands whether they are beginners or have large followings.

It appears to be aimed primarily at creators on platforms such as TikTok, Instagram, and similar social networks. Compensation may be a fixed payment, free products, or affiliate revenue, depending on the campaign.

If you received an unsolicited message from MyyShop, verify that links lead to the official myyshop.com domain and avoid paying upfront fees or sharing sensitive account credentials.
