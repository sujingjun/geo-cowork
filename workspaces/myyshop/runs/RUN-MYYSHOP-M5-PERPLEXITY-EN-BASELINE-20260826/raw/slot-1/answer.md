MyyShop is an AI-powered creator-marketing platform that connects social-media creators with brands for collaborations and monetization opportunities. Creators can find sponsored content campaigns, promote products, earn affiliate commissions, and use tools such as MyyFinds and free value reports.

It is aimed at creators ranging from beginners to large influencers and supports opportunities across areas such as fashion, beauty, lifestyle, and other consumer categories. Some campaigns may pay for posted content, while others offer products or commissions based on sales.

MyyShop also has an iPad app listed in Apple’s App Store. Like any creator platform, review each campaign’s requirements, compensation, product-shipping terms, and privacy or payment conditions before participating.
