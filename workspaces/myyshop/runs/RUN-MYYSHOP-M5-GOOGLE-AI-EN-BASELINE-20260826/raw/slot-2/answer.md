MyyShop is an AI-driven global creator marketing platform launched by the DHgate Group that connects content creators with brand collaboration opportunities.

Legitimacy and User Perspectives

Opinions on Reddit are mixed regarding MyyShop's user experience. Some users report completing successful paid collaborations and earning cash payouts. Other users report app or website glitches, navigation issues, and limited customer support.

Key Features

Zero-Barrier Entry: Allows users to sign up and join campaigns with zero followers.

AI-Powered Matching: Uses algorithms to pair creators with matching brand campaigns across platforms like TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube.

Revenue Opportunities: Offers free product samples, content creation fees, and cash payouts with no platform commission fees.
