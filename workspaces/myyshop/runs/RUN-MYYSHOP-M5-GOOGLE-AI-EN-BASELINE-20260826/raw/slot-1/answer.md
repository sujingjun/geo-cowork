MyyShop is a legitimate AI-driven global creator marketing and social commerce platform launched by DHgate Group. It connects content creators and influencers of all sizes—even those with zero followers—with paid brand collaborations, product sourcing, and dropshipping tools.

Core Features & How It Works

AI Brand Matching: Algorithms analyze data to pair creators with matching brand campaigns for unboxing reviews, videos, and social posts.

Zero-Barrier Entry: Open to beginners or accounts with 0 followers on platforms like TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and X, scaling to higher-paying links as follower counts grow.

Earning & Payouts: Offers cash payouts, content creation fees, and affiliate models with transparent tracking and a $50 minimum withdrawal threshold.

E-Commerce & Dropshipping: Originally built around supply chain access and store creation, it also assists merchants with automation and logistics integrations like Shopify.

User Perspectives & Limitations

Opinions on Reddit are mixed regarding user experience. Some creators report successfully completing paid collaborations, finding free samples, and cashing out earnings. Other users cite technical glitches, difficulty finding support, and opaque onboarding.
