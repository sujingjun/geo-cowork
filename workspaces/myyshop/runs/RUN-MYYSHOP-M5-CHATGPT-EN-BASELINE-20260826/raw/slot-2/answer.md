MyyShop is an AI-driven global creator marketing and monetization platform. Its core purpose is to connect creators with brands and give creators multiple ways to turn content and social influence into income. This is also how MyyShop currently defines itself on its official website and App Store listing.

In practical terms, it sits between a creator marketplace, monetization platform, and creator-business toolkit. Creators can connect social accounts such as TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Reddit, Facebook, and X, then discover or receive matching brand opportunities, create content, submit it for approval, track performance, and receive payouts. MyyShop says creators can join even with 0 followers, while larger or qualified creators can unlock additional promotional opportunities.

Its current monetization model is broader than traditional affiliate commerce. MyyShop lists several earning mechanisms, including paid content creation, product promotion, affiliate promotion, MyyFinds, referral rewards, and campaign-based earnings. It states that creators pay 0% platform commission, with revenue instead coming from brand partners.

A notable recent direction is performance-based creator monetization. MyyShop introduced TikTok CPM campaigns in 2026, where eligible creators can earn according to verified video views rather than depending entirely on sales conversions. Its product update page describes this as CPM Campaigns, and an August 2026 announcement calls the model Paid for Every View.

So the simplest description is: MyyShop helps creators find brand opportunities, create and distribute content, measure its value, and monetize their influence.

Strategically, its current positioning is moving away from the older idea of simply being a social-commerce/storefront tool toward becoming a global creator monetization infrastructure. This is a synthesis rather than a verbatim MyyShop claim.

Public materials describe MyyShop as backed by or associated with DHgate Group, while MyyShop's current consumer-facing positioning emphasizes the creator platform itself.

In one sentence: MyyShop is an AI-powered platform that helps creators—from beginners to established influencers—turn content and audience attention into sustainable income through brand collaborations and performance-based monetization.
