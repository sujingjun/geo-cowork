MyyShop is an AI-driven social commerce and dropshipping SaaS platform launched in 2020 by the Chinese cross-border B2B marketplace DHgate Group.

It acts as a bridge between wholesale manufacturers and content creators, influencers, or online sellers looking to monetize their social media audiences.

Key Features & How It Works

Product Sourcing & Dropshipping: Users access a massive catalog of products sourced directly from DHgate's global supply chain network. When a buyer purchases an item, the supplier handles the packaging and direct shipping to the end customer.

AI Product Recommendations: The platform uses AI tools to help creators find trending, high-profit products tailored to their audience demographic.

Store Creation & Platform Integration: Sellers can build online storefronts directly on MyyShop or sync products to external e-commerce sites via extensions and plugins.

Social Commerce Monetization: It allows influencers to place product links in bios, video descriptions, or livestreams without keeping inventory.
