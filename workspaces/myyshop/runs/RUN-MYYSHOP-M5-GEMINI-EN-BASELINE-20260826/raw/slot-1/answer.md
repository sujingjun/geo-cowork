MyyShop is an AI-driven social commerce and creator marketing platform launched in 2020 by DHgate Group. It bridges global suppliers (primarily Chinese manufacturers) with social media influencers, micro-creators, and digital entrepreneurs to monetize online traffic through dropshipping, affiliate marketing, and brand collaborations.

Core Features & How It Works

AI-Powered Creator Matching: Brands use AI tools on the platform to match their products with creators whose niche, audience demographics, and engagement style align with campaign goals.

Dropshipping & Store Creation: Users can quickly build customized storefronts or integrate MyyShop’s catalog directly into existing channels like Shopify, eBay, TikTok Shop, or private social media groups. Orders placed by end-consumers are automatically fulfilled and shipped directly from suppliers.

Cross-Border Supply Chain Integration: Built on DHgate’s B2B infrastructure, the platform provides access to millions of wholesale-priced products, global warehousing, automated fulfillment, and smart logistics management.

TikTok & Social Commerce Optimization: MyyShop specializes in multi-channel commerce, offering features like product seeding, livestream shopping tools, affiliate links, and automated content distribution across platforms like TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook.

Monetization Options for Creators

Creators join MyyShop for free and can earn money through multiple revenue streams.

Affiliate & Sales Commissions: Earn a percentage on completed product sales generated via social store links or campaign tracking.

Impression-Based Earnings (CPM / MyyFinds): Monetize views directly rather than relying solely on finalized sales. Programs like Paid for Every View allow approved TikTok creators to earn set rates.

Dropshipping Margins: Set custom retail prices on curated store items and keep the markup difference above the wholesale supply cost.
